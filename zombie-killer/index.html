Free to play. Single-player. Windows PC.

The mission

A soldier survives a mid-flight bombing and crash-lands alone in zombie-infested territory. Fight through the horde to find and rescue your teammates.

Kills power the signal

Every kill strengthens your signal. At full strength it reveals the location of one teammate, so the only way forward is through them.

Pick your weapons

Make strategic weapon choices and listen closely: immersive 3D audio tells you what’s coming.

Overkill Protocol DLC

A paid expansion that requires the base game. It adds three new levels in Tokyo, the hill roads of northern India and Brazil, drivable vehicles and a combat robot that fights alongside you.
