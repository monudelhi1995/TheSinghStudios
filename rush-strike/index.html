RUSH
STRIKE

Arcade combat racing for Android. Grab power-ups, blast the car ahead and fight your way to the top of the Night Circuit.

Get on Google Play ↗Watch the trailer ↗

Free on Android. Contains ads. Optional cosmetic packs never change car stats.

A story worth racing

You are Rudra Singh, a street racer with everything to prove. Battle 9 rivals across 5 acts and 25 story events, outrun the VCPD in high-speed pursuits and take on Adrian Vale for the crown.

Power-up warfare

Hold up to three power-ups and fire them forward or backward.

  • Shunt: a homing orb that flips the car ahead
  • Bolt: rapid energy shots
  • Barge: a shockwave that shoves everyone away
  • Shock: EMP domes dropped in front of the leader
  • Mine: throw it ahead or leave it behind
  • Shield, Repair and Nitro to stay in the fight

Takedowns and pack racing

Ram rivals off the road, ride the slipstream and nail the perfect nitro. With 10 cars on the grid, every race is a brawl from the first corner to the line.

39 cars to collect

Hypercars, supercars, muscle legends, JDM icons and off-roaders. Fit performance mods, then make them yours with paints, wraps, wheels, spoilers, underglow, nitro flames and tyre smoke.

21 locations

Neo Kanto, the Thames, Riviera cliff roads, Glacier Rift, Volcano Isle, the Dragon Wall, Golden Mirage, Carnival Hills and an orbital ring above a planet. Rain, thunderstorms and snow change the road.

20 street jobs

Time Trial, Wreck, Elimination and Takedown Hunt. Earn stars, fans and credits to open the next act of the story.

Race your friends

Race friends online or on the same Wi-Fi network.

Made for mobile

Simple thumb controls, optional auto-accelerate and quick 3–5 minute races. Play in English or Hindi.
