NovelFlix Publisher

NovelFlix Publisher is an internal tool made by TheSinghStudios. It uploads the short Hindi story videos we produce to our own accounts:

  • YouTube: Novel Flix ji
  • Instagram: novel_flix_ji

Who can use it

The tool is not open to the public and has no sign-up. It only connects to accounts operated by TheSinghStudios.

Why it asks for Google access

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload our videos and to confirm it is connected to the correct channel. See the privacy policy for how that access is handled.
