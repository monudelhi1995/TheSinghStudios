Privacy Policy

NovelFlix Publisher · Last updated: 9 November 2026

NovelFlix Publisher is an internal tool operated by TheSinghStudios ("we", "us"). It posts videos to the YouTube and Instagram accounts that we operate. It has no public sign-up and is not offered to other users.

Data the tool accesses

  • YouTube upload access (youtube.upload), used to upload our videos.
  • YouTube read access (youtube.readonly), used to read the channel ID, title and handle so we can confirm the tool is connected to the correct channel.
  • Instagram basic profile and publishing access, used to confirm the account and publish our videos.

The tool does not read the private data of viewers or of any other account.

How the data is used

Data is used only to publish our own videos and to confirm the connected account. It is not used for advertising, and the tool does not use cookies or analytics.

NovelFlix Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage

Access tokens are stored in files on computers controlled by TheSinghStudios. They are not stored on a public server. Tokens are kept until they expire or access is revoked, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

Sharing

We do not sell this data or share it with third parties, except when required by law. Videos we publish are sent to YouTube and Instagram, and become public on those services.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Google access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account connections page. Instagram access can be removed under Settings › App website permissions. To request deletion of stored data, email us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
